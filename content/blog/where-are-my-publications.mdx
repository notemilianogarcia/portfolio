Quick update: I now have two full papers accepted at **IEEE EMBC 2026**. This post is now a short overview of those publications while I finish preparing the final publication pages and proceedings links.

## Current publications

1. **Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks for Alzheimer's Disease Detection using Multimodal Speech Analysis**
	- **Authors**: Emiliano Garcia, Emad A. Mohammed
	- **Status**: Full paper accepted (IEEE EMBC 2026)

2. **Analyzing Coronavirus Cough Sounds and Alzheimer's Speech with Deep Learning Models to Discover Common Patterns**
	- **Authors**: Emad A. Mohammed, Salma J. Ahmed, Emiliano Garcia Ochoa, Behrouz H. Far, Amir Sanati Nezhad
	- **Status**: Full paper accepted (IEEE EMBC 2026)

## What this means
